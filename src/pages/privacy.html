3. Why we use it (and our legal basis)

  • To reply to your request and prepare a quote. Legal basis: your consent and steps you asked for before entering a contract.
  • To deliver your website if you become a client. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • To keep invoices and tax records. Legal basis: legal obligation.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam. Legal basis: our legitimate interests.
  • To send studio news, only if you ticked the optional box. Legal basis: consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link or by emailing us.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

5. How long we keep it

  • Requests that don’t become projects: deleted 12 months after our last contact.
  • Client project records: kept for the length of the project plus 2 years.
  • Invoices and tax records: kept for as long as tax law requires (usually 6–7 years).
  • Newsletter list: until you unsubscribe.
  • Host security logs: 30 days.
  • Your cookie choice: until you clear your browser storage, or until we update our Cookie Policy and ask again.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or the California Consumer Privacy Act and similar US state laws), you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy of it;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting anything done before;
  • not be treated differently for using any of these rights.

To use any of these rights, go to our Data Requests page or email scottyshopstore@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days (45 days where California law applies). We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will only ask for what we need to do that. You can also authorise an agent to make a request for you.

We honour Global Privacy Control browser signals as a valid opt-out.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk). In the EU, it is the supervisory authority in your country. We’d appreciate the chance to put things right first.

7. Children

This website and our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18, and our request form asks you to confirm you are 18 or older. If you think a child has sent us personal data, please contact scottyshopstore@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

When we build websites for clients that are aimed at children, we build them to meet the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR’s rules on children’s consent, and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code. That includes verifiable parental consent where required.

8. Security & international transfers

This site uses HTTPS encryption. Access to your data is limited to the people who need it, and we protect our accounts with strong passwords and two-factor authentication. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach happens that puts you at risk, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

Some of our providers may process data outside your country. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as adequacy decisions or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will highlight it on this site or email any clients it affects. Questions? Email scottyshopstore@gmail.com or call (920) 573-0659.
