The details

We want cancelling to be as easy as signing up. To cancel, just email hello@example.com with your name and project, and say you want to cancel. You don’t need to give a reason, fill in a form or make a phone call.

Cancelling the $75 monthly cost

Email us at any time to cancel. You won’t be charged again, and the service continues until the end of the month you’ve already paid for. There’s no cancellation fee. We don’t refund part-months, unless the law requires it or we failed to provide the service.

If we don’t deliver

If we miss the agreed launch date by more than 30 days for reasons that are our fault, or if we can’t deliver what was agreed, you can cancel and get a full refund of any amount for work you haven’t received.

Consumer cooling-off rights

If you are a consumer in the EU or UK, you have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of accepting your quote. If you ask us to start work during those 14 days and then cancel, we may keep a proportionate amount for work already done. This policy never reduces your statutory rights.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 days of your cancellation. There are no cancellation fees or processing fees. Third-party costs you paid directly to other providers, such as a domain registration, are refunded under those providers’ own policies.
