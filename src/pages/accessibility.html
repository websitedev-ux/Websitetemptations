Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: 28 September 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

What we’ve done

  • Colour contrast: all text meets or exceeds the 4.5:1 contrast ratio in both light and dark mode. Our main text measures about 14:1 or more.
  • Keyboard navigation: every link, button, form field and the cookie banner can be used with a keyboard alone. A clearly visible outline shows where you are, and a “Skip to main content” link appears on the first Tab press.
  • Alt text: meaningful images have text alternatives. Purely decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Forms: every field has a visible label. Required fields are marked in text as well as with colour, and errors are described in words and announced to screen readers.
  • Structure: pages use proper headings, landmarks and lists, and set the page language.
  • Light and dark modes: use the sun/moon button in the header. Both modes meet the same contrast standard. On your first visit, the site follows your device’s setting.
  • Motion: we respect your “reduce motion” setting.
  • Zoom: pages work at 200% zoom and on small screens without losing content.

Known limitations

If you choose to send a request by email (when our form opens your email app), accessibility depends on your email program.

Report a problem

If you find a barrier, please tell us at hello@example.com or on [phone number]. We aim to respond within 5 business days. We will also accept your website request in any format that works for you.
