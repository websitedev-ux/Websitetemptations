Questions

Good to know

What isn’t included in the price?

Only things you pay to other companies directly: your domain name, hosting, and any subscriptions you choose, such as a booking platform. We list the expected costs in your quote and never mark them up.

Work outside the agreed scope is quoted separately. It only goes ahead if you approve it in writing. There are no setup fees or admin fees.

The only recurring charge is the $75 monthly cost shown on every package. It starts when your site goes live, is billed once a month, and you can cancel it anytime by email with no cancellation fee.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the project. Your quote includes a timeline with dates, so you know exactly when to expect each stage.

Do I really own my website?

Yes. Once your final payment is made, the design, content and files are yours. We hand over every login. Fonts and plugins from other companies stay under their own licences, and we explain those to you.

What if I change my mind?

Send us one email. If design work hasn’t started, you get a full refund if you cancel within 7 days of accepting your quote. After that, you only pay for work already done, and you keep that work. See our Refund Policy.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo if you have one, the text and photos you’d like to use, and your feedback at each stage. We can write content for you on the Curated package.

What happens to my details?

We only use them to reply to you and prepare your quote. We never sell them, and you can ask us to delete them at any time. See our Privacy Policy or make a data request.
