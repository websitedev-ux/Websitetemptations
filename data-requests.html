Delete or access my data

You can ask us to delete your personal data, give you a copy of it, correct it, or stop using it for marketing. It’s free, and we reply within 30 days. We will only ask for extra information if we need it to confirm who you are.

What happens next

  1. We confirm we’ve received your request within 5 business days.
  2. We check it’s really you, usually by replying to the email address you gave.
  3. We complete your request within 30 days and tell you when it’s done.

When you ask us to delete your data, we delete it from our systems and ask our service providers to do the same. The only exception is anything the law requires us to keep, such as invoices. If that applies, we tell you what we kept and why. We keep a minimal record that your request was completed, so that we can prove we met it.
