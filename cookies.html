The details

Cookies and similar technologies (such as your browser’s “local storage”) are small pieces of data stored on your device. We keep them to a minimum.

What we use

Cookies and storage used on this site
NameCategoryPurposeStored for
wt_cookie_consent (local storage)Strictly necessaryRemembers your cookie choice so we don’t ask again on every page.Until you clear it, or we update this policy
wt_theme (local storage)Strictly necessaryRemembers whether you chose light or dark mode. It is only saved when you press the mode button.Until you clear it

That’s all. This site uses no analytics, advertising or social-media cookies. The “Preferences” and “Analytics” options in our cookie banner are reserved. They stay off unless you switch them on, and nothing in those categories runs today. If we ever add a tool in those categories, we will list it here first and ask for your consent again.

Third-party content

Our typefaces load from Google Fonts. This sends your IP address and browser details to Google so the fonts can be delivered, but it does not set cookies. See our Privacy Policy.
